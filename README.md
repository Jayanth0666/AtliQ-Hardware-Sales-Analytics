# 📊 AtliQ Hardware Sales Analytics Project Using Excel

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales data for AtliQ Hardware, a manufacturing company that sells products through multiple channels including retail stores and third-party customers.

The goal was to transform raw, unstructured data into meaningful insights that can support business decision-making.

Using Excel as a Business Intelligence tool, I built a complete data pipeline — from data cleaning to data modeling and visualization.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- Power Pivot (Data Modeling)
- Pivot Tables (Data Visualization)

---

## 📌 Key Business Questions Answered
- Which customers are driving the most revenue?
- Which products contribute the most to growth?
- Which markets are underperforming vs targets?
- What are the top-performing regions and divisions?

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 💰 Overall Business Growth
- Revenue grew from **$196.69M in 2020 to $598.88M in 2021**
- This represents a **204.5% increase**, showing aggressive growth scaling  
(*Source: Customer Net Sales Report - Page 2*)

---

### 🧑‍💼 Customer Performance
- Major contributors include:
  - Amazon
  - AtliQ Exclusive
  - AtliQ e Store
- Some customers showed **300%–600% growth**, indicating strong demand expansion  
(*Source: Page 1 & 2 reports*)

---

### 🌍 Market Performance vs Target
- Overall business missed targets by **~8.4%**
- Countries like:
  - USA
  - India
  - Canada  
  showed strong revenue but still underperformed against targets  
(*Source: Market Performance Report - Page 3*)

---

### 📦 Product Performance
- Top 10 products delivered **708% growth**
- Products like:
  - AQ Smash 2
  - AQ Electron Desktop Processor  
  were key revenue drivers  
(*Source: Top 10 Revenue Drivers - Page 4*)

---

### 🏢 Division-Level Insights
- PC division grew by **313.7%**
- P&A division contributed the highest revenue overall  
(*Source: Division Level Report - Page 5*)

## 🔍 What I Did

### 1. Data Cleaning (Power Query)
- Removed inconsistencies and null values
- Standardized formats across datasets
- Prepared clean tables for modeling

### 2. Data Modeling (Power Pivot)
- Created relationships between multiple tables
- Built a structured data model
- Enabled efficient aggregation and analysis

### 3. Data Visualization (Pivot Tables)
- Built interactive dashboards
- Created reports for:
  - Customer Performance
  - Market vs Target
  - Product Analysis
  - Division Performance

---

## 💡 Key Learnings
- Excel can function as a powerful BI tool when used with Power Query and Power Pivot
- Data modeling is the backbone of accurate reporting
- Small mistakes in relationships or aggregation can drastically impact results
- Business insights are not just numbers — they drive real decisions
